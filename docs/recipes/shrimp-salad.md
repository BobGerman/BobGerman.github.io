# Shrimp Salad with Baby Peas

## Ingredients

 - 1 lb salad shrimp (91-120 legs and tails removed)
 - 1 scant cup frozen baby peas, thawed
 - 1/2 cup finely chopped red onion
 - 1/2 cup finely chopped red bell pepper
 - 1/4 cup finely chopped celery
 - 2 tsp lemon juice
 - 2 tsp dill weed (dried)
 - 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
 - 1/2 cup - 3/4 cup Duke's mayonnaise (to desired consistency)
 
## Instructions

1. Thaw and drain shrimp and peas
2. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl
3. Refrigerate for a few hours or more
