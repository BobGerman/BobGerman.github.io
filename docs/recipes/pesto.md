# Bob's Pesto

## Equipment

 - Food processor
 - 2-cup Pyrex measuring cup

## Ingredients

 - 2-cup Pyrex measuring cup packed with basil leaves (about 3 cups
 - 2-4 medium cloves of garlic
 - 1 cup shelled walnuts
 - 1/2 cup grated parmesan or romano cheese
 - good olive oil
 - salt and pepper to taste
 
## Instructions

1. Chop the garlic in the food processor
2. Add the basil and chop thoroughly
3. Add the nuts and cheese and blend throoughly
4. Drizzle olive oil while blending until it reaches the desired consistency
5. Briefly blend in salt and pepper

## Variation
Use cilantro instead of basil
