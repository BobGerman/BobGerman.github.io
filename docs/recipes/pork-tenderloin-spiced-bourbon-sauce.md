# Pork Tenderloin in Spiced Bourbon Sauce

## Ingredients

 - 1/4 cup Kentucky Bourbon
 - 1/4 cup soy sauce
 - 1/4 cup brown sugar
 - 3 cloves garlic, minced
 - 1/4 cup Dijon mustard
 - 1 teaspoon fresh ginger root, minced OR 1/4 teaspoon powdered ginger
 - 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
 - 1/4 cup vegetable oil
 - 2 (1-lb each) pork tenderloins

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients, except pork with a whisk or in processor.
2. Place tenderloin and marinade in a ziploc bag in the refrigerator overnight.
3. Cook 4 inches from a hot charcoal fire for 15 to 25 minutes or until the pork has reached 145-150°F internally and has just lost its pinkness. Baste while cooking. Do not overcook or it will be dry. (The meat may also be broiled 6 inches from the heat for 16 to 18 minutes.) Baste often.
4. Slice in 1/2-inch thick slices to serve as a main course or slice in 1/4-inch slices to put on top of French bread for a picnic. Serves 6.
